# Project rules

- Scheduling: the AI only requests bookings via markers ([AGENDAR]/[REMARCAR]/[CANCELAR_AGENDAMENTO]); `src/lib/scheduling.server.ts` validates, reserves and writes the customer confirmation. Why: prevents false confirmations.
- Booking conflicts are enforced by the `agendamento_sem_sobreposicao` exclusion constraint (company + recurso + time range, active statuses) and idempotency by `agendamento_idem_uniq`; never rely on a pre-check alone. Why: concurrent webhook calls.
- The internal `agendamento` table is the source of truth; Google Calendar is synced afterwards with a deterministic event id and `google_sync_status` for reconciliation. Why: Google and the DB are not one transaction.
- Pure scheduling rules live in `src/lib/scheduling.ts` and are covered by `src/lib/scheduling.test.ts` (`bun run test`). Why: testable without network/DB.
- Booking duration comes from the matched service: duration ranges ("60 a 75 min") use the maximum, an empty "Serviços agendáveis" falls back to "Produtos/Serviços" items that state a duration, and ambiguous service names are not guessed. Why: a short default duration let bookings overlap the next customer.
- Appointment reminders (1 day / 1 hour before) are sent by the managed schedule calling `/api/public/jobs/send-reminders`; each reminder is claimed in the DB (`lembrete_*_enviado_em`) before sending and released on failure, and replies ("1"/"2"/"cancelar") are handled in the WhatsApp webhook before opt-out. Why: avoids duplicate reminders and keeps "cancelar" from pausing the contact.
- Billing/credit columns on `company` (status_cobranca, trial_ate, creditos_*, slug, financeiro_ativo) and `subscription` are written only by the server (service role) or super admin; the `company_guard` trigger rejects browser changes. Why: owners could otherwise unlock paid access themselves.
- Team integrity is enforced by the `company_user_guard` trigger (only owners touch owner rows, at least one active owner, members may only clear their own forcar_troca_senha). Why: admins/attendants could remove or demote the owner.
- The `anon` role has no table grants except `plan`; credit RPCs (`consume_ai_credit`, `topup_plan_credits`, `seed_fin_categorias`) are service-role only; Google OAuth tokens are column-revoked from `authenticated`. Why: least privilege for the public Data API.
- Outbound webhook URLs must pass `checkOutboundUrl` in `src/lib/security-utils.ts` (https, no internal hosts, no redirects) on save and before each send. Why: prevents SSRF from the server.
- Cron-called routes under `/api/public/hooks|jobs` must call `authenticateCronRequest` first. Why: these run with service role.
